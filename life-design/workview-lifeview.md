## Workview

I view work as primary source of accomplishment, it allows me to exercise my intellect and skills in a productive way. I like to feel that I am skilled or good at some thing.

I view work as a way to do something tangible and useful to someone, I dont have strong opinion on its scope though.

It is also an activities for me to connect with others, and have a shared sense of honour. Today, I still remember the time where I achieved something together with friends.

I also view it as a way for me to growth. It can change over time, it can be absent sometime in life, but I cant imagine living a life completely devoid of it.

I think grit is important in work, achieving something should not come easy and if its too easy it probably isnt that valuable, at least to one self.

## Lifeview

Accomplishment is important to me, I used to think I need something big in my life for it to be considered a well-lived life.

I am less adamant on this, probably because I've suffered and grow older, I am now less energitic, less naive and sometimes I feel tired. But in most day I still view life positively, and still believe we can all do better.

One of my favorite quote is about life, from Romain Rolland
> There is only one heroism in the world: to see the as it is, and to love it.

After years of living abroad, I also appreciate connections with people more than I would ever thought of when I was young, I seek connection, I seek understanding, and I seek company. Its importance become obvious after they are no longer in abundance. The most important connection comes from my partner, I like to be treated well and it brings out the better version of me, I am generally reciprocal.

Throughout the years, I also learned something about me, I value rules and predictabilities, I dislike conflict when myself is involved.

I want to live a healthy, energitic life. I think I can only live a good life being a doer, creator, if I am live a passive life, it is likely a miserable one.
I admire people that are obsessed about certain thing, its something I used to have but the spark has since gone from my life.

I used to think the indicator of an awesome life is one where I am always excited to wake up and live the day, I am less sure now, that might simply be sign of anxiety.
I still a good life is a life with good amount of desire, with wants beyond survival.

## Integrations

Both my life and work views emphasis on achievement, on being outstanding and good at something. I also seek interactions with people in both aspect.
Both views also put a lot of emphasis on myself, as opposed to others, it suggests I seek something from within and does not think as much on a broader scope like the world, the society or the community.

I also value doing and working, putting effort into something in both views.

I dont see much of a clash in my views.

I see these 2 aspects drive each other interactively, with maybe my lifeview driving workview, since its part of my core belieft that achievement is important to me, and work happens to be a venue that I can consistently get them.
